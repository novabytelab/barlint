Privacy Policy

Barlint: 2D Barcode Verifier (Android app in.novabytelab.barlint)
Publisher: NovaByte Lab · Effective date: 16 October 2026

In short: the app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you scan is processed and stored on your phone. We (NovaByte Lab) do not receive any of your data. The app only goes online when you ask it to, as described below.

What the app processes on your phone

DataWhyWhere it stays

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this. You can also clear the history inside the app.

When the app connects to the internet

  • Resolver check (only when you tap it). The app sends a request to the web address encoded in the GS1 Digital Link you scanned, to see whether the link works. That request goes directly to the brand or service that owns the link, includes the product identifiers in the link, and reveals your device's IP address to that server, as any web request does. Their privacy policy applies to that request.
  • GS1 rule updates (only when you tap it). The app downloads the public GS1 Barcode Syntax Dictionary file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). No personal data is sent; GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are bought, renewed and cancelled through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your payment details. The app reads your purchase status from Google Play on your phone.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data, because we never receive it. When you export a PDF certificate, a CSV file or an expense receipt, the app hands the file to the Android share sheet and you choose where it goes.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes.
  • Internet: only for the resolver check, GS1 rule updates and Google Play billing described above.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
